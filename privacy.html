NursePiece Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the NursePiece website (nursepiece.com) and NursePiece Pin Factory, the Pinterest app made by NursePiece.

Who we are

NursePiece is a small print-on-demand t-shirt business that sells on Etsy. NursePiece Pin Factory is an internal tool used only by NursePiece. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app does

NursePiece Pin Factory posts pictures of our own Etsy products to our own Pinterest business accounts, and creates boards on those accounts. Every account it uses belongs to NursePiece and was connected by NursePiece through Pinterest's official sign-in (OAuth).

What data we use

  • Basic information about our own Pinterest accounts (account name), our own boards and our own Pins, only to post and organize our Pins.
  • The access tokens Pinterest gives us after sign-in. They are stored only on NursePiece's own computer and are never shared.

We do not collect, store or process data about other Pinterest users. We never ask for or store any Pinterest password.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.

Website visitors

nursepiece.com uses no cookies, no forms and no tracking. Our web host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Deleting data

Any account owner can remove the app's access at any time in Pinterest settings (Security and logins, apps). When access is removed, we delete the stored tokens for that account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.

Contact
